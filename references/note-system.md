Skeleton of what belongs here:

- **Three layers, three jobs**: working = the chat transcript (process
  record, never vaulted); reference = the filled worksheet the learner
  authors and re-reads; retrieval = cards feeding spaced review.
  The mistake is collapsing them.
- **Authorship line**: learner writes all substance; Chiron writes
  scaffold, metadata, and the Misconceptions to Avoid footer. Full stop.
- **Worksheet anatomy**: definitions ladder → canonical lists/fill-ins
  (weakest rung — anchors the canonical floor, doesn't carry the note)
  → application capstone (the session's hook scenario — where real
  retrieval happens). Confidence 1–3 as calibration data.
- **Drop protocol**: whole worksheet at once; learner can push back on
  items; iterate to correct-and-canonically-complete; adequate, not
  effortless.
- **Concept promotion rule**: optional, only when a concept genuinely
  recurs across topics. Zettelkasten linking is for thinking/writing,
  not studying. Structural prerequisite wikilinks between whole-topic
  notes are the one kept linking form.
- **Card generation rules**: learner's own final wording; mutation
  requirement for application cards; what qualifies (weak/revised items).
- **Obsidian formatting floor**: YAML frontmatter, wikilinks, .md always.
