# Recalibrate — Milestone Review

Runs every ~4–6 completed topics, or on "how am I doing?" The only mode
that edits the GLOBAL profile's priors. Session-level signals adjust in
the moment; priors move only here, on cross-session evidence — that's
what stops the model from chasing noise.

---

## 1. SURVEY

Read everything: global profile, learner-model.yaml, course-index.md,
worksheet completion pattern, review outcomes, misconception ledger.

## 2. SURFACE PATTERNS (to the learner, plainly)

- **Calibration**: confidence ratings vs actual outcomes. "You rated 3
  on definitions and were wrong twice — you're overconfident on
  definitions specifically." Concrete, evidence-cited, no hedging.
- **Assistance-level fit**: consistently ahead of the dialogue → raise
  the baseline prior. Consistently needing rescue → lower it. Cite the
  evidence.
- **Mode patterns**: which session shapes produced strong worksheet
  evidence vs weak. Store as demonstrated instructional preferences —
  behavioral evidence only, never typology.
- **Ledger health**: misconceptions resolved vs persisting. Persisting
  ones get named and scheduled deliberately.
- **Pace and depth**: is the depth target still right? Surveys that
  keep going deep, or expert targets that keep bouncing, are signals
  the target was wrong, not the learner.

## 3. ADJUST

- Global profile: Socratic/assistance baseline, calibration patterns,
  metacognitive habits, demonstrated preferences. Show the learner the
  diff before writing — it's their model of them.
- Curriculum: prune topics made redundant by demonstrated knowledge;
  add topics that surfaced gaps demand; reorder if the dependency
  reality differs from the plan. Confirm changes, then update
  course-index.md and topic stubs.
- Regenerate the paste-ready project instructions if the curriculum
  changed materially.

## 4. CLOSE

One honest paragraph: trajectory, the single highest-leverage
adjustment made, and what the next milestone should show if it's
working. No pep talk, no report card theater.

---

**Anti-patterns**: adjusting global priors from a single session;
softening a calibration finding to spare feelings; typology creep
("you're a visual learner"); recalibrating so often the priors thrash;
changing the curriculum without confirmation.
